An open archive of the world’s sound

Planet Sound keeps field recordings at the place and the moment they were made. Anyone can add one. Nothing is reconstructed or invented: every entry is a sound someone stood in and chose to keep.

How entries are kept

Recordings made here are captured without the phone’s voice processing (no echo cancelling, noise reduction or automatic gain) and stored losslessly as 24-bit FLAC. Each entry carries its own catalogue card: where and when, who recorded it and with what, and the measured facts of the file — sample rate, bit depth, integrated loudness, peak level and the background level of the place.

Playback is level-matched, so moving between a quiet forest and a loud market doesn’t throw you. The file itself is never altered.

Taking part

You don’t need an account. Your first recording gives you a listener key: it proves the recordings are yours, so only you can edit them, and it lets you open your archive on another device. Keep it somewhere safe; it can’t be recovered if lost.

You choose, per recording, whether it joins the public map or stays in your private archive, and whether others may download it.

Care

Please don’t publish private conversations or anything recorded without consent. Entries are curated: the archive keeps the right to remove anything that doesn’t belong here.
